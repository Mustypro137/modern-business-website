A small workshop that finishes what it starts

We build tables, chairs and shelves one at a time, from boards we choose ourselves. Every joint is cut by hand and every surface is finished by hand.

Nothing leaves the workshop until we would be happy to own it. If something is wrong when it arrives, we fix it.

How an order works

  1. Tell us what you need. The piece, the wood and the size you have in mind.
  2. Get a drawing and a price. We send both, and the price is fixed before we start.
  3. We build it. Made to order in the workshop, usually in six to eight weeks.
  4. We deliver it. Brought to your home and set up where you want it.
